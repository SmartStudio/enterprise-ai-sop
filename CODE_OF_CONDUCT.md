# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

This is a public methodology repo. We expect contributors and commenters to stay professional, specific, and free of harassment.

## Standards

- Critique the SOP text, not the person
- No harassment, slurs, or sexualized language
- No spam, SEO stuffing, or “star this” threads
- No sharing of client data, keys, or vendor accounts

## Enforcement

Report abuse to contacts@fxai.ai. Maintainers may edit or close issues, lock threads, or block accounts. We will not debate harassment in public threads.
